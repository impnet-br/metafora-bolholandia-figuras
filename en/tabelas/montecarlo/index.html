Feasibility study of the Successive Zooms Method. 180 configurations · 20 000 sessions each · precision 20 digits.

How to read. Three noisy devices read each block; if they disagree, the monitor orders a reprocess until there is unanimity. The value is recovered almost always — the residual failure is the rare wrong unanimity (the three agree on the same wrong value), which the majority-of-3 does not detect. d is the number of digits read per round; more digits = fewer rounds, at the cost of a more expensive device.
